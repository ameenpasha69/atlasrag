# Data Retention Policy

This policy governs how long operational records are kept in hot storage before they are
moved to cold storage or destroyed.

## Operational records

Dispatch logs are retained for 30 days before archival. Courier location pings are retained
for 14 days. Customer contact records are governed by a separate policy and are out of scope
for this document.

## Rationale

The retention window balances the cost of hot storage against the operational need to
investigate recent delivery disputes. Disputes raised more than a month after delivery are
resolved from archived records instead, which is slower but substantially cheaper.

## Exceptions

An explicit legal hold suspends destruction for the records it names, and a hold takes
precedence over every window in this document. Holds are recorded in the compliance register
and reviewed quarterly.
