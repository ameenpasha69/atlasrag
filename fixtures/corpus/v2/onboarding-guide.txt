Meridian Logistics Operations Onboarding

Welcome to hub operations. This guide is plain text on purpose: it is printed and pinned in
the crew room, and it must remain readable without any formatting.

Your first week

You will shadow a floor supervisor for three shifts before taking an independent station.
During shadowing you are expected to learn the intake door controls, the staging belt stop
switches, and the exception lane procedure. You are not expected to memorise route tables,
because route assignment is handled by the dispatch system.

Safety

Never reach across a moving belt. Every belt has a stop switch within two metres of any
working position, and using it is always the correct first response to a jam. Report every
stop switch activation to the supervisor, even when the jam clears immediately, because
repeated activations at the same position indicate a mechanical fault.

Reporting

Shift notes are entered before you leave the floor, not afterwards from memory. A shift note
records exception lane volume, any belt stoppages, and crew changes. Supervisors review the
previous shift notes during handover.

Who to ask

Questions about equipment go to the floor supervisor. Questions about pay, leave, or
scheduling go to the hub administrator, who is not on the floor and is reached through the
crew room terminal.
